# 02. Key Messaging

## Positioning Statement

The Livingstone College STEM Programme positions students to excel as a well-informed citizenry in the global STEM community by providing a holistic education that leverages innovative instruction, early engagement in research, cutting-edge technologies, state-of-the-art facilities and transformational mentorship.

## Brand Story

### A Legacy of Hope and Empowerment
Founded in 1879 as Zion Wesley Institute, later becoming Livingstone College, the institution has a long history of empowering African Americans through education and STEM excellence.

### Historic Grant Rejuvenates STEM
In 2021, the programme received a $2.24 million National Science Foundation grant to strengthen STEM retention, persistence and graduation outcomes.

### F. George Shipman Science Annex
Completed in 2022, the facility includes:
- Research laboratories
- Planetarium
- Immersion theater
- Classrooms
- Hydroponic greenhouse

### Holistic Education
Built around:
- Innovative instruction
- Early research engagement
- Transformational mentorship

## Elevator Pitch

The Livingstone College STEM Programme prepares students to excel globally through innovative instruction, research opportunities, leading-edge technology, state-of-the-art facilities and transformational mentorship.

## Boilerplate

Livingstone College provides a comprehensive STEM education focused on innovation, research engagement, technology, facilities and mentorship to prepare students for success in the global STEM community.
